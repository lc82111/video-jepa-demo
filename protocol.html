↗ Evaluation protocol

What was held fixed, what was changed, and what was measured.

This page records the protocol behind the interactive companion. It is intentionally compact; the repository contains the full run manifests and logs.

01control evaluation

100 paired Push-T cases

Initial states, goals, case ordering, and selection seed 42 were held fixed across planner comparisons.

02tested horizons

H100 and H150

HADP uses the locked directional weights λ=2 at H100 and λ=1 at H150. Continuous interpolation was not validated.

03model comparison

Same learned dynamics

Native CEM and HADP rank the same candidate terminal latents. HADP adds a directional cost to the planning score.

04dynamics evaluation

Epochs 1 through 10

Direct next-latent and residual prediction are compared with next-latent MSE, autoregressive rollout AUC, and temporal path straightness.

05uncertainty

Episode bootstrap intervals

Reported intervals capture paired case variation. They do not replace uncertainty over training seeds; the current evidence uses one training seed.

06reference clips

MP4s are labeled honestly

The synchronized comparison is browser-rendered from representative traces. The available MP4s are recorded reference clips from the A5 run, not paired Native/HADP replays.
