04 LDAD · retain action information

Make the latent displacement explain the action.

LDAD adds an inverse-dynamics-style training signal to the RLD displacement. It is not an extra inference module: the decoder is discarded before CEM planning.

TRAINING-ONLY ACTION SIGNALdt = zt+1 − zt
dt→Dψ(dt)→ât

The decoder sees the change, not the absolute latent. At test time, the decoder leaves the graph and CEM uses the learned model as usual.

07 Findings & boundaries

What the paper supports—and what it does not.

The final section is deliberately plain: the components are complementary in the design, but not every combination is a clean win.

01RLD

Improves early transition learning.

RLD reaches 34% vs 16% Push-T success at epoch 1 and improves temporal path straightness.

02LDAD

Makes displacement action-aware.

H5 action-ranking accuracy rises from 84% to 93%; the decoder is training-only.

03HADP

Changes 40.9% of CEM decisions.

Directional cost falls while the learned model stays fixed; point estimates are positive at both tested horizons.

04uncertainty

The long-horizon interval crosses zero.

The isolated A1 + HADP aggregate CI is [−2, +10] pp: promising, not statistically established.

05not validated

Continuous horizon interpolation was not tested.

The locked weights are λ=2 at H100 and λ=1 at H150; do not interpolate without new evaluation.

06scope

Current evidence uses one training seed.

Episode bootstrap intervals do not replace uncertainty over training seeds.
