06 Findings & boundaries

A map of what the evidence does—and does not—say.

The page is designed to make the caveats legible at the same zoom level as the wins.

01supported by this study

Residual prediction improves optimization and early control.

At epoch 1, A1 has lower rollout AUC and higher straightness than A0 on the recorded one-seed trajectory.

02positive point estimates

HADP is positive at both tested horizons.

A1 + HADP reaches 18.7% at H100 and 16.7% at H150, compared with 13.3% and 14.0% for native CEM.

03factorial evidence

The contributions operate independently.

A19 reports no residual × directional-planning interaction: +0.0 pp, 95% CI [−7.3, +7.0].

04not validated

Continuous horizon interpolation was not tested.

The locked weights are λ=2 for H100 and λ=1 for H150. Do not generalize between them without new evaluation.

05not consistent

RLD/A14 and HADP did not consistently stack.

The transfer result moves from 15.3% → 19.3% at H100 but 13.3% → 12.7% at H150.

06scope of evidence

Current evidence uses one training seed.

Episode bootstrap intervals do not replace multi-seed uncertainty. Treat the result as a promising lead.
